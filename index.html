MEGA·CV

A modular audio and video synthesizer for the browser, patched with a pin matrix.

  • Rows of the matrix are outputs, columns are inputs. Click a pin to connect.
  • Audio and CV are the same signal. Any audio output can drive any video parameter.
  • Drag knobs up and down. Double-click to reset.
